Epsilon Indi, Risa and Kepler-174 include Guardian-revisited Mirror history.
Their illustrations and current populations are game interpretations. Harlak's
bombarded base does not imply the entire globe was destroyed. Gorlan's homeworld
is destroyed; Gorlan Station is a separate location.

Ventar comes from Bareil's embellished account. Betazed, Tellar, Denobula and
Coridan adapt homeworld geography from references to their peoples; the dialogue
does not establish their complete physical systems. Dannus V, New Bajor, Pahvo,
Kwejian, Archer IV, Archer's Planet, Beta III, Eminiar VII and Sherman's Planet
are treated as Prime-derived counterparts, not visits to Mirror planets in those
episodes. C-111 contains Beta III and is one destination, not duplicate systems.

## Kepler-174 astronomy

NASA lists three transiting planets around a K-type primary. These numbers are
reference data, not the flight-map scale. They do not establish inhabited surfaces.

| Planet | Radius (Earth) | Orbital radius (AU) | Period (days) | NASA class |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| [b](https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanet-catalog/kepler-174-b/) | 1.96 | 0.100 | 14 | Super Earth |
| [c](https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanet-catalog/kepler-174-c/) | 1.49 | 0.214 | 44 | Super Earth |
| [d](https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanet-catalog/kepler-174-d/) | 2.19 | 0.677 | 247.4 | Neptune-like |

The fictional Mirror art/culture account is retained alongside these measurements.
The temperate artwork for d is a fictional surface interpretation; it is not an
observed rocky landscape. b and c are placed inside d in the compressed chart.

## Existing systems and assets

Ferenginar chooses one licensed Ventarus Idrilon scheme; its additional surface
types are authored. Hof-Maso's extra neighbors and exchange/fleet operations are
original game content. Clarus and Irtok are separately referenced Ferengi-area
systems, with original operating stations and uninhabited survey neighbors.

The [implementation notes](../../system-expansion.md) list working shipyards,
all ten generated assets, prompt/provenance locations and actual verification.
Source badges are available in the atlas; gameplay panels show the destination.
