How the comparison works +

Danube stays at its current in-game size. Corrected visible length = reference length in meters ÷ 23.1 × Danube’s current visible hull length. Every sourced hull uses this same conversion. Zoom scales the whole lineup together.

The current column measures the painted hull inside its game sprite. The smaller “sprite” number is the full flight frame, including transparent padding. Danube retains both its visible hull and full frame dimensions; corrected frame dimensions for other ships account for their own padding. Artwork proportions are preserved; reference beam widths and heights are not reconstructed.

Published dimensions sometimes disagree, and screen shots are not always consistent. Each entry links to the chosen source and records important alternatives. Provisional values are marked. Custom designs with no verified length stay at their current size and are marked “Unverified”; they are not assigned an invented canon length.

This page reads the shared roster and ship definitions. It does not run the galaxy simulation, write saves, or change gameplay. Gameplay adoption would also need matching collision geometry, weapon positions and navigation clearances.
