WHAT CHANGED

A closer look at the improvements.

The showcase makes each part of the upgrade easy to inspect.

01

A complete ship roster

32 requested classes have rebuilt artwork matched to your approved fleet references. The original 66 classes have four damage stages; Tug Class II-A has four damage stages, live lights, flying hull pieces and breach fires; the other 11 Tug Class II variants have intact artwork and live lights. The game uses compact textures; separate 8K masters are upscaled exports.

02

Damage that stays aligned

Hull art changes below 75%, 50% and 25%. The ship keeps the same position and size across all four stages. Repairs restore its healthier appearance.

03

Weapon hits you can read

Brief beam flashes, spinning torpedoes, colored bolts, shield tint and hull sparks follow the upgraded game's effect formulas. Shield and hull readouts show each ship?s original capacities. Hit results report shield absorption and hull damage.

04

A more detailed destruction

Explosions burst across the ship's silhouette. Rebirth debris spins outward, slows and fades, with the original explosion audio available through the sound button.
